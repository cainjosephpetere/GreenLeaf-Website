FAQ

Questions, answered simply.

Frequently asked questions help visitors find information without having to contact the organisation for every small detail.

Is Green Leaf a real organisation?

No. Green Leaf is a fictional organisation created for the website project.

Can I order products online?

The prototype presents a product catalogue but does not process real payments or orders.

Does Green Leaf offer workshops?

Yes, workshops are included as a proposed service for community groups, schools and teams.

How do I ask about a product?

Use the Enquiry page and select “Product enquiry”.

Where is Green Leaf based?

The fictional organisation has proposed contact locations in Cape Town and Johannesburg.

What is the website's main goal?

To provide useful information, clear navigation and simple ways for visitors to engage with Green Leaf.
